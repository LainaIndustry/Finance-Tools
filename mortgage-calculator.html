Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mortgage? ▼
A mortgage is a loan used to purchase a property, where the property itself serves as collateral. The borrower makes regular payments over a fixed period to repay the loan.
What is the ideal down payment for a mortgage? ▼
The ideal down payment is typically 20% of the property price, which helps avoid private mortgage insurance (PMI). However, some lenders accept lower down payments, especially for first-time buyers.
What is the difference between a mortgage and a home loan? ▼
A mortgage is a type of secured loan specifically used for purchasing real estate. Home loan is a broader term that includes mortgage loans and other loans used for home-related purposes like renovation.
