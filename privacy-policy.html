Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Loan & Finance Calculators, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our services:

  • Calculator Inputs: The values you enter into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • Analytics Data: We may use basic analytics to understand how our website is used and improve user experience.
  • Cookies: We may use essential cookies to maintain your preferences and improve site functionality.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our calculator services
  • To improve and optimize our website user experience
  • To analyze usage patterns and trends
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All calculations are performed locally in your browser, and we do not store your financial data on our servers.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Google AdSense for advertising. These services may collect information about your browsing behavior to display relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of your personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of data collection for analytics

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
