Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Recurring Deposit (RD)? ▼
A Recurring Deposit is a type of savings account where you deposit a fixed amount monthly for a specified period. It offers higher interest rates than regular savings accounts and helps build a disciplined savings habit.
How is RD interest calculated? ▼
RD interest is calculated using the compound interest formula for regular deposits. The interest is typically compounded quarterly, and the maturity amount is calculated using the future value of annuity formula.
What is the minimum deposit for RD? ▼
The minimum deposit for a Recurring Deposit varies by bank, but it's typically as low as ₹500 per month. Some banks allow even lower amounts, making it accessible for all income groups.
