Frequently Asked Questions

What is a lump sum investment? ▼
A lump sum investment is a one-time investment of a large amount of money. Unlike SIP where you invest regularly, lump sum involves investing a single amount at once.
When is lump sum investment better than SIP? ▼
Lump sum investment can be better when you have a large amount to invest and the market is favorable. However, it comes with higher risk as the entire amount is invested at once.
How is lump sum return calculated? ▼
Lump sum returns are calculated using the compound interest formula: A = P × (1 + r)^n, where P is the initial investment, r is the rate of return, and n is the number of years.
