Frequently Asked Questions

How is loan interest calculated? ▼
Loan interest is calculated using the EMI formula which takes into account the principal amount, interest rate, and loan tenure. The total interest is the difference between the total payment and the principal amount.
What affects the total interest on a loan? ▼
The total interest on a loan is affected by three main factors: the loan amount, the interest rate, and the loan tenure. Higher loan amounts, higher interest rates, and longer tenures result in higher total interest.
How can I reduce the total interest on my loan? ▼
You can reduce total interest by: (1) Making a larger down payment to reduce the loan amount, (2) Choosing a shorter loan tenure, (3) Shopping for lower interest rates, and (4) Making extra payments when possible.
