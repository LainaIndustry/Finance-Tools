Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average return on investment? ▼
The average return on investment varies by asset class. Historically, stocks have returned 8-12% per year, bonds 4-6%, and real estate 5-8%. Past performance doesn't guarantee future returns.
How do I calculate my investment returns? ▼
Investment returns can be calculated using the compound interest formula: A = P × (1 + r)^n, where A is the future value, P is the initial investment, r is the rate of return, and n is the number of years.
What is a good rate of return? ▼
A good rate of return depends on your investment goals, risk tolerance, and time horizon. Generally, an 8-12% annual return is considered good for long-term investments in stocks or mutual funds.
