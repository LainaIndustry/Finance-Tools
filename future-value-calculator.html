Frequently Asked Questions

What is future value? ▼
Future value is the value of an investment at a specific point in the future, taking into account compound interest and regular contributions. It helps you project how your money will grow over time.
How is future value calculated? ▼
Future value is calculated using the compound interest formula that includes both the initial investment and regular contributions. The formula factors in the interest rate, time period, and compounding frequency.
Why is future value important? ▼
Future value is important for financial planning as it helps you set realistic savings goals, understand how investments grow over time, and plan for major financial milestones like retirement or education.
